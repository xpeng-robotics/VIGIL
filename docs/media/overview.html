About the recordings and evidence

The dedicated fridge, apple and kettle sequences retain all 5, 6 and 25 recorded actions. Playback is accelerated, not a measurement of model latency. Apple and kettle use the labeled action-feedback intervention. Task illustrations are separate from continuous recordings.

The film describes the original paper dataset: 1,000 episodes, with 265 iTHOR and 735 ProcTHOR tasks. The executable release includes later task corrections. The 640 × 480 observations retain their original detail; no intermediate scene images are generated. Music is original synthesized audio.
