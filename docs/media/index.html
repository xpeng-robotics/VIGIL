About the footage

These are two separate qualitative, controlled-intervention replays. Both retain every source observation and model decision. The source labels both terminal reports as false success with trace-only validity; this is not an independently certified hardware outcome or a model ranking.

Faces are obscured in the film, its covers and all four full hardware recordings. Progress indicators show playback position and decision indices, not task-completion percentages. Music is synthesized for the edit; the observations are sampled steps rather than elapsed robot time.
